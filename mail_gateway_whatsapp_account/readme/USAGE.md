To send an invoice by WhatsApp:

1. Open a **posted** invoice (customer invoice, credit note, vendor bill or
   vendor credit note).
2. Make sure the partner has a **phone** or **mobile** number set.
3. Click on **Send by WhatsApp**.
4. Select the WhatsApp gateway and optionally an approved WhatsApp template.
5. Click on **Send**.

The invoice PDF is generated and attached to the message automatically.
